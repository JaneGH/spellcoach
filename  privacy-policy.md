# Privacy Policy

SpellCoach ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

The App may collect limited technical and usage information to improve performance and user experience, including:

SpellCoach is an educational application designed to help users improve English spelling, vocabulary, and language skills through interactive learning exercises.

The App may use third-party services such as analytics platforms, cloud providers, or AI services to provide app functionality and improve learning experiences.

These services may process limited technical data according to their own privacy policies.

Examples may include:

* Firebase
* Google Play Services
* AI language processing services

## Children's Privacy

SpellCoach is intended for general educational use. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by law.
